Baby Chart

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Baby Chart is a small app for parents to log their baby's feeds, sleep, nappies, temperatures and medication, and to share that log with their partner. It's made by a parent for family and friends. It isn't a commercial product.

What the app stores

  • Your account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored by Google Firebase in scrambled (hashed) form and can't be read by anyone.
  • Your children's details: the name and date of birth you enter for each child.
  • Your log: the feeds, sleeps, nappy changes, temperatures, medication and notes you record.
  • On your device: app settings such as alert preferences, and a sign-in token so you stay signed in.

Where it's kept and who can see it

Everything is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), a cloud service run by Google. A family's chart can only be read or changed by the accounts that belong to that family: the person who started it, and anyone they share their invite code with.

As the operator of the app's Firebase project, the developer can technically access the stored data through Google's console. It's never looked at except to fix a problem you've asked for help with.

What the app doesn't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Notifications

The phone apps can show a reminder when a feed is due. These reminders are scheduled on your own device and aren't sent through any server.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and family › Delete my account in the app. This deletes your sign-in. If you're the only member of your chart, it also deletes the chart and every child's log. You can also email the address below to ask for your data to be deleted.

Health information

The tips in the app are general information for healthy babies and aren't medical advice. Always follow your midwife, child health nurse or doctor.
