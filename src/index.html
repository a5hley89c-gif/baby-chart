When to call the GP or 13 HEALTH
  • Fewer wet nappies than her day of life in the first 5 days (2 on day 2, 3 on day 3), fewer than 6 heavy wet nappies after that, or dark, strong-smelling wee.
  • Too sleepy to wake for feeds, or fewer than 8 feeds in 24 hours in the first weeks.
  • Temperature of 38°C or more while Baby is under 3 months old. See a doctor straight away.
  • Pale or white poo, blood in poo, green vomit, or poo still black and sticky after day 4.

13 HEALTH (Queensland): 13 43 25 84, any time. Call 000 if Baby is floppy, blue or struggling to breathe.
